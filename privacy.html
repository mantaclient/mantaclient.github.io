Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what Manta Client ("Manta", "we") collects and how it is used. Manta is a Windows launcher plus a Minecraft mod; most of what it does stays on your own PC.

Microsoft sign-in

Signing in opens Microsoft's own sign-in page in a separate window. Manta does not read or store your Microsoft password. Microsoft returns a sign-in code, which Manta exchanges with Microsoft, Xbox Live and Minecraft services for a Minecraft access token and your Minecraft profile (username, UUID and skin).

Manta saves the Microsoft refresh token and the Minecraft access token on your PC, encrypted with Windows (DPAPI) so only your Windows user account can read them. They are used only to launch Minecraft and for features you use that need your Minecraft account, such as changing your skin. These tokens are never sent to Manta's server, never sold and never shared with anyone else.

Friends and parties

Friends and parties use a Manta server hosted on Cloudflare. To prove who you are, the launcher sends the server your username, UUID and the public part of your Minecraft player certificate, which is signed by Mojang. No Microsoft or Minecraft token is sent. The server stores:

  • Your Minecraft username and UUID
  • Your friends list, friend requests and blocked players (their usernames and UUIDs)
  • Your online status (offline, in the launcher or in game), when it last changed and, unless you turn it off, the server address you are playing on
  • Parties you are in, and party invites (invites expire after one hour; a party is deleted once everyone leaves)
  • Your friends privacy settings

Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver requests and may keep it briefly in standard logs. We do not use it to track you.

Data that stays on your PC

The following is stored only on your computer and is not sent to us:

  • Equipped cosmetics, quest and achievement progress, and settings
  • Clips: if you turn clips on, the last minute of gameplay video and Minecraft's audio is recorded to a buffer on your PC while the game runs; it is saved only when you press the clip hotkey
  • Screenshots, session recaps, FPS reports and network checks (pings to your router and to 1.1.1.1), including which programs are using your CPU while you play
  • "Now playing": if you turn it on, the song shown in Windows' media controls is read so it can be shown in game

Other services Manta contacts

To work, Manta talks directly to these services. Each has its own privacy policy.

  • Microsoft, Xbox Live, Mojang and Minecraft: sign-in, game files, skins and looking up players' usernames
  • Fabric and Modrinth: mod loader and mod downloads
  • mcstatus.io: the status of servers in your server list (the server address is sent)
  • MCPVP, PvPTiers, PvPHQ and mc-heads.net: the Stats page looks up a player's public stats and skin render by username or UUID
  • OneDrive: checking for and downloading Manta updates
  • gyan.dev and GitHub: one-time downloads of FFmpeg (for clips) and PresentMon (for FPS measuring) if you use those features

Manta has no analytics, ads or crash-report uploads.

How long we keep data

Server-side data is kept until you ask us to delete it. Sign-ins with the Manta server expire after 30 days.

Your choices

  • Sign out in the launcher settings to delete your saved tokens and profile from your PC.
  • Turn on "Appear offline", or turn off showing your server, in the friends panel.
  • Ask in the Manta Discord to have your server-side data deleted.

Children

Manta is not directed at children under 13. Players must follow Microsoft's and Mojang's account rules.

Changes

We will update the date at the top of this page when this policy changes.
