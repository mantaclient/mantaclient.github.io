Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what Manta Client ("Manta", "we") collects and how it is used.

Microsoft sign-in

You sign in on Microsoft's own page; Manta never sees your password. Microsoft gives Manta an access token and a refresh token, which Manta exchanges with Xbox Live and Minecraft services to get your Minecraft profile (username, UUID and skin) and a game token.

These tokens are stored only on your own device so you stay signed in. They are used only to launch Minecraft and show your profile in the launcher. They are never sent to Manta's servers, never sold and never shared with anyone else.

Online features

Friends, parties and cosmetics use a Manta server. To prove who you are, the server checks your account with Mojang's session server; it never receives your Microsoft or Minecraft tokens. The server stores:

  • Your Minecraft username and UUID
  • Your friends list, friend requests and blocked players
  • Your online status and, unless you turn it off, the server you are playing on
  • Party membership and invites (invites expire after one hour)
  • Your privacy settings and equipped cosmetics

Like any website, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes your IP address to deliver requests and may keep it briefly in standard logs. We do not use it to track you.

How long we keep data

Online-feature data is kept while you use Manta. Sign-in sessions with the Manta server expire after 30 days. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Your choices

  • Sign out in the launcher to delete stored tokens from your device.
  • Use "appear offline" or hide your server in the launcher settings.
  • Email us to have your server-side data deleted.

Children

Manta is not directed at children under 13. Players must follow Microsoft's and Mojang's account rules.

Changes

We will update the date at the top of this page when this policy changes.
