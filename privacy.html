Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

What we access

When you sign in with Microsoft, Manta receives an authentication token used to sign in to Xbox Live and Minecraft services, plus your Minecraft username, UUID and skin.

How it is used

Tokens are used only to launch Minecraft and to show your profile in the launcher. Tokens are stored on your own device and are never sold or shared with third parties.

Online features

If you use friends, parties or cosmetics, your Minecraft username and UUID are sent to the Manta server to provide those features.

Deleting data

Sign out in the launcher to remove stored tokens from your device. To request deletion of server-side data, contact us via the repository's issue tracker.
