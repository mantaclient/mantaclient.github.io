Squad up. Drop in together.

Make a party of up to 8 friends right in the launcher. When the leader hits Play or joins a server, everyone who follows is taken straight there. No more typing IPs into chat.

  • Invite any friend from your friends list with one click
  • Live status for everyone: in launcher, launching, in game
  • Follow leader: the launcher starts your game into their server, or switches you over if you're already playing
  • Your party shows on the in-game menu and server list

Your menu, your client.

Manta replaces the title screen with one that knows you: quick-join your servers, see your party, and open every Manta mod with Right Shift.

  • Built-in performance and quality-of-life mods, each with its own settings
  • Movable HUD, including Minecraft's own hotbar, chat and scoreboard
  • Server quick-join right from the main menu

Add mods in one click.

Browse Modrinth from inside the launcher. Manta only shows mods that work with your version, checks every file before install, and keeps each profile separate.

  • Profiles for every version, each with its own mods and packs
  • Texture pack browser and organiser with previews
  • Every mod scanned for known malware before launch

Microsoft sign-in

Manta uses Microsoft sign-in only to authenticate your Minecraft account so you can launch and play, the same way the official launcher does. Sign-in happens on Microsoft's own page; Manta never sees or stores your password. Your sign-in is kept encrypted on your PC and you can sign out at any time.
